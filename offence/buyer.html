1. Find your suppliers

No suitable offers? Keep your policy intact and try another seed. The app never sends prompts to an ineligible supplier.

2. Set your rules

Select models above, then save. The agent uses the alias auto. It cannot change these rules.

A latency ceiling excludes unmeasured suppliers. Context is a supplier claim; this version accepts at most 16 KiB of text input. Suppliers receive your full prompt. The default requires trusted supplier keys. Verify those keys through an independent channel: discovery signatures and low prices do not establish trust. Choosing unknown suppliers explicitly removes this protection.

3. Control payment

Advanced: direct LND connection

Use the local wallet environment described in the download README, then select LND and save to verify the network.

Amounts below are in sats. All limits start at zero. Delivered output is charged even if a later batch fails.

Fractional token charges accumulate across chunks and round up by less than one millisatoshi per request. The one-cent deposit is credit, not a flat fee. The per-request cap also limits deposits. For prepaid LND, fee reservations cover one funding payment. Legacy per-batch settlement requires enough for one batch per output token. LND fee caps count toward the daily allowance. For NWC, both LND fee fields must be zero; the wallet controls fees. Unused quote allowance is released when a purchase stops. Dispatched payments with unknown outcomes stay reserved until wallet recovery. Received-output cost above excludes routing fees and payments whose output could not be recovered.
